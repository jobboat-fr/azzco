VIGIL — Multi-LLM council architecture

VIGIL is an AI meeting copilot. Its core design principle: no single model is trusted alone. Every intervention is drafted, reviewed and voted on by a council of models from different families before it reaches the user.

The council pipeline

  1. Ingest — a real-time meeting transcript flows into the AI Worker Collective.
  2. Primary worker — Claude Sonnet, specialized per role (CFO / Tech / Legal / Product advisor), drafts a proposed intervention.
  3. Reviewers — GPT-4o and Gemini Flash score the draft on six dimensions in structured JSON. Different model families are used deliberately, to avoid bias collapse.
  4. Consensus vote — a weighted vote must pass a 66% consensus threshold and an 80% readiness gate.
  5. Chairman fallback — if consensus fails, a chairman model synthesizes the final answer.

In parallel, the Behavioral Pattern Engine (550+ patterns across 50 ensembles) reads each speaker's transcript window and emits live signals: stress, evasion, learning agility, persona alignment.

VTLVS — Universal wireless charging pad

VTLVS (pronounced Atlas) is a single hardware SKU that wirelessly charges every class of electric vehicle. The design bet mirrors USB-C: win by being one abstraction over every device.

Four revenue models, one pad

  • B2C retail — direct sale for homes and buildings.
  • B2B parking — Hardware-as-a-Service for parking operators.
  • B2G municipal — curbside charging concessions for cities.
  • B2B2C hospitality — charging as an amenity, billed via SaaS.

One SKU across all four collapses stock, supply chain and support — the strategic insight behind the product.

JobBoat — Career platform

JobBoat merges three experiences that today live in separate apps: inspiring career content, professional networking, and job discovery with genuine profile understanding.

  • Web platform plus native mobile apps (Expo + React Native, iOS & Android).
  • Profile-aware recommendations — matching driven by deep profile understanding rather than keyword search.
  • Behavioral engine lineage — the pattern-analysis technology developed for JobBoat became the foundation of VIGIL's Behavioral Pattern Engine.
  • GDPR-first — user data powers recommendations and is never resold.

Our delivery method (services)

Every client engagement — automation, agentic installation, systems, web or data — follows the same auditable path:

  1. Diagnose — free consultation; we map workflows, data and constraints, and rank automation candidates by leverage.
  2. Design — a written architecture and rollout plan: scope, guardrails, costs, success metrics. You approve before any build.
  3. Deploy — short iterations, human-in-the-loop gates on anything customer-facing, audit logs on every automated action.
  4. Operate or hand over — monthly operation by us, or a documented handover your team can run without us.

Design principles we do not compromise on: deterministic routing (rules decide which system handles what — not vibes), prepare-only vs. execute separation (analysis environments can never send or write to production), and cost governance (per-run budgets, monitored).
