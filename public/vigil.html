One meeting in. Three verdicts. One intervention.

Single-model AI assistants hallucinate confidently. VIGIL doesn't get that chance.

Step 1

Primary worker

A real-time transcript flows in. Claude Sonnet, acting as your CFO, tech, legal or product advisor, drafts an intervention.

Step 2

Reviewer council

GPT-4o and Gemini — different model families to avoid bias collapse — score the draft on six dimensions, in structured JSON.

Step 3

Consensus gate

A weighted vote must clear a 66% consensus threshold and an 80% readiness gate. If it fails, a chairman model synthesizes the final answer.

The Behavioral Pattern Engine

In parallel with the council, VIGIL reads each speaker's transcript window through 550+ behavioral patterns across 50 ensembles — surfacing real-time signals about stress, evasion, learning agility and persona alignment.

  • Know when a negotiation counterpart is evading
  • Spot stress and misalignment on your own team early
  • Signals delivered live, during the meeting — not in a recap

Learns your job to back you up

VIGIL builds a long-term memory of your decisions, style and context (RAG over your own history) so its interventions sound like your best self on your best day.
