How one pad serves everything

Hardware simplicity is the strategy: one SKU collapses stock, supply chain and support.

⚖️

Weight-first classification

The pad identifies what parked on it by weight signature in under 200 ms — no RFID tag, no app, no connectivity required.

🧲

Four nested TX coils

Coils at r = 360 / 280 / 200 / 80 mm plus a solid-state relay deliver the right field to the right vehicle — a 90× power dynamic range from one pad.

🛡️

Built for the street

700 × 700 × 200 mm, IP68 waterproof, IK10 impact-rated. Designed for parking lots, curbsides and hospitality forecourts.

Same pad, four markets

The strategic insight: one hardware platform, four business models running on top.

B2C

Retail

Home and building owners buy the pad outright for daily wireless charging of bikes, scooters and cars.

B2B

Parking — Hardware as a Service

Parking operators deploy pads on a HaaS model, monetizing every spot for every vehicle type.

B2G

Municipal concession

Cities install curbside charging without per-vehicle-type infrastructure — one universal layer for public space.

B2B2C

Hospitality SaaS

Hotels and venues offer charging as an amenity, managed and billed through the VTLVS software layer.
